Aim with the net

Every catch bounces your jumper back into the air, and where they land on the net decides where they go next.

Left side Center Right side

Left side

Back toward the fire. The building bounces them back, so it’s not over yet.

Center

Nearly straight up. Safe and slow, and handy for holding one jumper while you deal with another.

Right side

A long throw toward the ambulance. Fewer bounces, more risk.

Get someone to the ambulance in two bounces or fewer for an Express bonus.

It gets harder. Fairly.

The net starts wide and forgiving, then narrows as your score climbs. A second jumper joins at 500 points, a third at 2,000, and everything speeds up as you go.

Extra jumpers only leap when you could actually reach them. If two land at once, it’s because of where you aimed.

Get it on your Playdate

Crank & Catch installs over Wi‑Fi from your Playdate account. It takes about a minute.

  1. Download the game.

    Get CrankAndCatch.pdx.zip. Don’t unzip it.

    Download for Playdate

  2. Make sure your Playdate is registered

    It needs to be registered to your account at play.date. Wireless installs only work with a registered device.

  3. Upload it.

    Sign in at play.date, open Account → Sideload, and drop in the zip.

  4. Open Game Library on your Playdate.

    The game downloads automatically; refresh the list if it doesn’t show up right away. On older system software, look under Settings → Games.

Prefer a cable? Panic’s guide covers installing over USB too: help.play.date. Sideloaded games stay in your account, so you can reinstall any time.
